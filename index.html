HOW RECONSTRUCTION WORKS

Motion instructions become closed beads.

GCode Rebuilder follows extrusion-positive G0/G1 moves and supported G2/G3 arcs, detects layers from Z, and gives each deposited segment a width and height. It joins those bead segments into a triangle mesh for practical inspection and export.

Will this recover my original STL exactly?

No. Slicing discards original surfaces and introduces printer-specific extrusion behavior. This tool reconstructs the sliced, deposited version represented by the G-code.

Which commands are supported?

G0/G1, G2/G3 (I/J arcs), G90/G91, M82/M83, G92, tool changes, and common slicer feature comments are recognized. Unusual commands are reported rather than silently ignored.

Where does my G-code go?

Nowhere. The parser, mesh generator, and exporter execute in your browser.
